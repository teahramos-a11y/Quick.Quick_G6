01Quick-Quick
Research Journey:

For our first step, we brainstormed different topics and content ideas that we could potentially stream. We discussed the advantages and disadvantages of each idea to determine which would be the most suitable and manageable for our group. Some of the initial suggestions included cooking or baking and gaming. After considering our options, we initially decided to explore Lifestyle Vlogs and Arts and Crafts. However, as we further evaluated the amount of preparation, resources, and effort required for each type of content, we decided to focus solely on Lifestyle Vlogs. We found this option more practical and easier for our group to organize and produce. We also considered the broad appeal of Lifestyle Vlogs, as this type of content is widely viewed and can attract audiences with different ages, locations, backgrounds, and interests. Additionally, Lifestyle Vlogs provide greater flexibility, allowing us to balance different activities and experiment with various topics while keeping the production process manageable. Ultimately, our final decision to focus on Lifestyle Vlogs was based on practicality, flexibility, audience appeal, and the amount of work required. Through brainstorming, comparing our ideas, and evaluating their advantages and disadvantages, we were able to develop our initial concepts into a clear and realistic final content decision.
